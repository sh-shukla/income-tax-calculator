Compliance, in order

  • TDS 194J — clients withhold 10% on professional fees. It's your money: reclaim via 26AS/AIS at filing.
  • GST — register at ₹20L receipts (₹10L NE/hill). Foreign clients? Register regardless — exports are zero-rated with LUT.

  • 44ADA lock (§58, ITA 2025) — opt out after using it and you're barred for 5 years. 50% is a floor, not a target.
  • 234B/C interest — missed advance tax costs 1% a month.
