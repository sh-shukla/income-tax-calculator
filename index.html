Contractor compliance checklist

  • TDS: clients deduct 10% u/s 194J on professional fees — it's pre-paid tax, claim it via Form 26AS/AIS when filing.
  • GST: register at ₹20L receipts (₹10L NE/hill states). Exporting services (foreign clients)? Registration required regardless — exports are zero-rated with LUT.
  • ITR form: ITR-4 for 44ADA, ITR-3 for regular books.
  • 44ADA fine print (§58, ITA 2025): once you opt out after using it, you can't return for 5 years. 50% is the floor — declare more if you earned more.
  • Interest: missing advance tax costs 1%/month u/s 234B/234C.
